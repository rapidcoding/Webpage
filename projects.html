Camera-Based Calorie Tracking App

I helped develop a calorie-tracking mobile app for a State Farm mobile app competition. The app used the phone’s camera to identify food and estimate calories to make tracking faster and more convenient.

  • Built the Android app experience using Kotlin.
  • Integrated a PyTorch-based model pipeline to classify food from camera input.
  • Collaborated on the overall feature design and end-to-end workflow for capturing, analyzing, and logging meals.

Tech: Kotlin, Android, PyTorch
